Questions people actually ask

Before you call.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the program and schedule, and we give you the number in writing on your first call. Federal aid, a payment plan, and scholarships can all apply. Nobody enrolls without seeing the full breakdown.

How long does it take?

Cosmetology is 1,500 hours, about 47 weeks full-time. Esthetics is 600 hours, about 19 weeks. Most programs finish in under a year.

Can I work while I'm in school?

Full-time students are on campus most of the week, and many still work evenings or weekends. Tell admissions your schedule and they will tell you honestly whether it fits.

Do I need experience?

No. You need a high school diploma or GED and the will to show up. The skills are what you are here for.

What about the state board exam?

Your hours, your practical work, and your theory are built around it. Ask admissions for the program's exam prep plan and pass history before you enroll.

I'm not sure which program.

Book a tour. Walk the floor, watch a class, and ask the students. Picking in person beats picking from a web page.
